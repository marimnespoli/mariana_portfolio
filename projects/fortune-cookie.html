Fortune Cookie

A fortune cookie app where one click breaks the cookie and reveals a random fortune.

Project Scope Web design and development
Skills Next.js · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · React · Figma · Illustrator

The Goal

I wanted to create an experience where the gesture of breaking the cookie was the center of everything — one click, one reveal. Nothing before, nothing after.

The challenge was making a simple interaction feel satisfying through animation and visual design. The entire app lives in a single gesture.

A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step
