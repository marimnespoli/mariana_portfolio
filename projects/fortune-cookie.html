Fortune Cookie

Interactive Fortune App

Overview

A delightful fortune cookie
experience in your browser

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, this app lets users crack open a fortune cookie and receive a randomly generated fortune. The interaction is playful and tactile — click to break, reveal to read.

The focus was on smooth animations, component architecture with React, and a clean UI that feels fun without being cluttered.

Design

What it is

A simple and playful web app where you crack open a fortune cookie and receive a random message. The focus is on the interaction — one click, one reveal.

Design process

Conceived and designed entirely in Figma, with custom illustrations created in Adobe Illustrator. The visual identity draws from Chinese New Year aesthetics — warm tones, decorative patterns, and hand-crafted assets.

How I built it

Built with Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS. The code was implemented with the help of Claude through the Figma MCP, translating the design directly into a working web app.
