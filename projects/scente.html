The Challenge

Design an editorial brand experience from scratch.

Scenté started as a brief to design and build a candle brand from scratch — from a full design system (color palette, typography, components) to three core pages: Home, Collection, and Contact. Every screen was designed and built across desktop, tablet, and mobile, with the editorial, warm aesthetic holding up consistently at every breakpoint.

The Design

Home, Collection, and Contact pages were fully designed and prototyped in Figma across desktop, tablet, and mobile — mapping out user flow, layout, and component consistency before development began.
